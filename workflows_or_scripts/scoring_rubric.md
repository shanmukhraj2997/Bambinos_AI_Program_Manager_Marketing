# Rubric and Creative Taxonomy

## Moment buckets

1. **Aha Moments:** A child demonstrates understanding or explains a concept in their own words.
2. **Curiosity Moments:** An interesting question, unexpected discovery or demonstration of curiosity.
3. **Problem-Solving:** A child solves a puzzle, challenge or problem.
4. **Creative Expression:** Storytelling, original ideas, imagination or communication.
5. **Interactive Learning:** Games, experiments, visual activities and teacher-child interaction.
6. **Confidence Moments:** A child volunteers, attempts a challenge or responds positively to feedback.

## Ten-point rubric

| Criterion | Range |
|---|---:|
| Parent learning value | 0–2 |
| Scroll-stopping hook | 0–2 |
| Child participation | 0–2 |
| Positive authentic emotion | 0–1 |
| Visual potential | 0–1 |
| Audio clarity | 0–1 |
| Sound-off readability | 0–1 |
| **Maximum** | **10** |

The system prompt says a candidate scoring 7 or higher can be shortlisted only if no hard exclusion applies. Transcript-only analysis cannot confirm visual quality or actual audio clarity; those must be marked pending until the recording is checked. A score never overrides a hard exclusion or consent gate.

## Hard exclusions

Reject or escalate child/family identifying information, embarrassment or distress, fear/discomfort, teacher shaming/threatening/insulting/humiliating behaviour, negative or inappropriate interaction, unverified outcomes, materially altered dialogue, private information visible in shared screens/documents, undocumented marketing consent, or material that cannot responsibly be cleared.
